Ask us directly →
Who is the Launch Package for?

It’s designed for people who have recently formed a UK limited company and want help with bookkeeping, annual accounts and routine company filings.

What does the £60 monthly fee include?

The package includes year-end accounts and Corporation Tax return filing, an annual confirmation statement filing, bookkeeping software, a dedicated accountant, VAT guidance and ongoing tax advice.

Do I need to know my filing deadlines?

No. Filing dates depend on your company’s incorporation and accounting period. We’ll help confirm the relevant dates for your company.

What happens after I get in touch?

We’ll review the details you share and contact you to discuss your company and the best next step.

Can I cancel?

There’s no setup fee, and the package can be cancelled at any time.
